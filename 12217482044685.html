Important:

All phases of this installation must comply with NATIONAL, STATE, and LOCAL CODES. In
absence of local codes, the installation must conform with American National Standard-Z223.1a- National Fuel Gas Code Latest Revision.

 

Requirements of Gas Heat

The unit gas train and optional through-the-base gas shut-off valve are rated at 0.50 PSIG maximum. A pressure-reducing regulator is recommended to prevent this maximum from being exceeded. These components must be isolated during a field gas piping test that exceeds 0.50 PSIG. It is recommended that the field piping be capped prior to the unit gas train or optional through-the-base gas shut-off valve if present.
