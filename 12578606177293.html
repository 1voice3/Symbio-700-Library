Using the Symbio 700 Onboard menu and keypad

  1. Press Home.
  2. Press the Down Arrow and select Utilities.
  3. Press the Check.
  4. Press the Down Arrow and select Unit Config.
  5. Press the Check.
  6. Press the Down Arrow and select Edit Unit Config.
  7. Press the Check.
  8. Press the Down Arrow, then select Demanded Controlled Ventilation.
  9. Press the Check.
  10. Press the Down Arrow, then select Enabled.
  11. Press the Check.
  12. Press the Down Arrow, then verify that the CO2 Sensor says Installed.
  13. Press the Down or Up Arrow until you find Save Config.
  14. Press the Check.
  15. Press the Down Arrow to change No to Yes.
  16. Press the Check.
  17. It will say Edit Unit Config Updating.

Possible Alarms

  • Space CO2 Concentration Input
    • Indicates the space CO2 concentration from a sensor hardwired to the controller. (Verify J22)
  • Space CO2 Concentration Active
    • Indicates the active space CO2 concentration being used by the controller.
  • Space CO2 Concentration Arbitrator
    • Indicates the space CO2 concentration being used by the controller.
