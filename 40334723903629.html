(b) In units with steam or hot water coils applied vertically or horizontally, check IOM for proper Isolator Kit selection.
(c) Do not use if blower will operate less than 600 RPM.
(d) When the air handler is in the vertical position and close proximity trapping of condensate is required, use of subbase is required.
