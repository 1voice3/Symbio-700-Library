Trane has a new licensing system for Symbio 700 controllers on Trane or American Standard units. You can use the new system to do the following:

  • Assign an Advance License to a basic controller.  More info
  • Download or email the license file for a controller. More info

Frequently Asked Questions

I see new terminology on the licensing screens. What do the new terms mean?

Asset: An asset is the license for the Symbio 700. Every controller installed on a piece of equipment should have an asset, whether it's basic or advanced.

License Entitlement: A licensed feature that's been purchased on that controller. Example: Advanced License

License Serial Number: The serial number for a license entitlement. You receive one of these by email when you purchase a license. 

Account: The owner of the equipment, controller, and license. 

License Entitlement Status: Available entitlements can be assigned to an asset. Active entitlements have been assigned to an asset.
