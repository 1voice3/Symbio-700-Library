Voyager 3 w/Symbio 700: Gas Heat E02/RED flash code of 51/Primary Limit Open

VoyagerTroubleshootingSensors / ModulesInstallation / StartupHeating
Section: V3 w/Symbio 700 controls  •  Category: Large Commercial RTUs | Splits | CSC
Created: 2025-02-17  •  Last updated: 2026-03-17

The Large Commercial Technical support team has received a number of calls on the Gas Heat Primary Limit Open diagnostic (E02 for the 1285 and a flash code of RED 51 for the 1287 staged).  If the Gas heat is a 600 MBH burner then there are 5 devices that are wired in series with the "High Limit" input (3 Gas Pressure switches, a Flame Rollout, and a High Temp limit).  The Pressure switches should all be set to ~5" iwc...the Supply/low gas pressure switch should open if the Supply Gas Pressure falls below 5" and the 2 manifold/high gas pressure switches should open if the manifold/leaving gas pressure from the switches rises above 5".  The input is a 24Vac input and so you can verify that the module output is 24 Vac and the input is receiving 24Vac.  For testing purposes ONLY you can temporarily add a jumper between the Orange wires (J7 pins 2 & 15 on the 1285 modulating board and J4 pins 9&10 on the 1287 Staged board).
