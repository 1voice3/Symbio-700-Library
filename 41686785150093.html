Symbio® 700 Indoor Options Module (IOM) Not Communicating

General Symbio 700TroubleshootingFirmware / SoftwareSensors / ModulesCommunications / BACnetInstallation / StartupHeatingManuals / Literature
Section: Modules / Sensors / Switches  •  Category: Light Commercial Rooftops | Splits
Created: 2025-12-05  •  Last updated: 2026-10-07

 

Troubleshoot: 

 

Does the IOM Module have power?

  • Verify that LED 8 is solid green in the middle of the module, and LEDs 5 & 6, located under the P4 connector, are lightly flickering. 
  • Verify IOM-P5 has 24VAC between Red and Black

  • Verify IOM-P5 has 3.2 - 3.9 VDC between Blue and Orange

     

     

  • If there is no power at IOM-P5, verify UC-P4/J4 on the main Symbio unit controller and trace it to the IOM.

 

 

Does the Factory-Installed module have the address plug?

 

  • The Indoor Options module, when factory-installed, includes a pre-installed address plug on J11. The factory plug is already configured for address 75. 
  • The rotary dials should be set to 00.

 

 

 

Is the Field-Installed module addressed?

  • The field-installed Indoor Options module must be addressed to address 75 on the provided rotary dials. 
  • SW1 is addressed to 7
  • SW2 is addressed to 5

 

  • Sometimes, you may need to turn the dial 360° to set the address. Cycle power or go to Tools > Service > scroll down to IMC LINK RESET (Phone App).

 

 

Is the UC module configured?

 

  • To verify the communication status, go to Tools > Service in your mobile phone app.
  • When it says Communicating, there should be no active alarms for the Indoor Options module.
  • When it says 'Communicating - Not Configured,' it means the Indoor Options module is communicating, but there is no configuration associated with it. This will show two active alarms.
  • You must configure the IOM under Settings > View Configurations > Edit.
