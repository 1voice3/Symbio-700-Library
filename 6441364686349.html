How to Update Time & Date

 

To change the time and date on the Symbio 700 mobile app, follow these general steps. Note that the exact steps may vary slightly depending on the version of the app and the operating system of your mobile device:

 

Open the Symbio 700 Mobile App:

  • Launch the Symbio 700 app on your mobile device.
     

Log In:

  • Log in by viewing available devices

 

Access Settings:

  • Look for a Tools icon at the bottom
     

Find Date and Time Settings:

  • Navigate through the Tools menu to find the option Regional Settings

 

Adjust Date and Time:

  • Select the option to change the date and time. You can set these manually or sync them with your mobile device's current date and time settings.
     

Save Changes:

  • After making the necessary adjustments, ensure you save the changes.
