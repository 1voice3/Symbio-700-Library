Trane Employees: Check Your Access to License Management in Salesforce

General Symbio 700General
Section: Symbio  •  Category: Licensing / Registration (Software & Hardware)
Created: 2026-06-30  •  Last updated: 2026-08-17

To verify whether you have access to License Management in Salesforce, follow the steps below and confirm that you can access the required functions.

  1. Click the link - Symbio 700 Asset | License
  2. Log in as a Trane Technologies employee.
  3. Verify that the Asset Details page is displayed.
  4. Verify that the following buttons are available:
  • Add License
  • Transfer License
  • Generate License File

If you can complete these steps successfully, you already have access to Salesforce License Management and do not need to submit a new access request.

If you are unable to complete the steps above or access the required functions, please submit an access request for License Management - How to Get Access to new Licensing System
