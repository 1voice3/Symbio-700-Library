Contractors & Customers: How to get Access to the new License Management portal

General Symbio 700General
Section: Service Tools  •  Category: Building Automation | Integration | Service Tools
Created: 2026-06-05  •  Last updated: 2026-07-09

This page explains how contractors and customers can register for an account in the Trane Controls Licensing portal

How to Register

  1. Click the registration link: User Registration
  2. Under Sign Up section, enter the following information:
  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Email Address
  • Password
  • Company Name
  • Trane Supply or Enterprise Customer Account Number (Optional)
  • Order Reference Number (Optional) - Use the Oracle Order Number from any recent Symbio 700 license order.
  • Address - Enter your Company Address
  1. Accept the privacy policy and click on [Submit]

Important: Please make sure all information is accurate and complete. Incorrect or missing information may delay the registration process

 

What Happens Next

  1. After you submit the form, you will receive a confirmation email acknowledging your registration request.
  2. Our team will review and process your registration in the background.
  3. Once your registration is approved, you will receive an email confirming that your Trane Licensing Account has been created. This email will also include your login credentials.

Processing Time: The registration process typically takes 1 to 3 business days, depending on the accuracy of the information submitted.

 

How to Sign In

  1. After you receive your account credentials, open the licensing portal.
  2. Click Sign In with SSO.
  1. Log in using the credentials provided in the registration approval email.

Additional Note: You can also change your password from the sign-in screen.
