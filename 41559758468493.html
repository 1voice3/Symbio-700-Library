Resolved - Service Alert: Voyager 3 & Precedent - (Symbio 700) Nuisance Alarming for "Invalid ID" or "Config Invalid" Diagnostics on Modulating Gas Heat Units

Service Alert Summary:

Modulating gas heat units may produce multiple nuisance alarms reporting "Diagnostic: Modulating Gas Invalid ID Plug Manifold 1" and "Diagnostic: Gas Heat Lockout Manifold 1" after updating to Symbio 700 firmware version 7.02

Info about Service Alerts

What are known Diagnostics or Symptoms:

The initial issue was identified as originating from the manner in which the Symbio 700 managed the routine reboots of the VB1285 Ignition Controller. The initial fix in v7.02 changed how Symbio 700 handled these alarms, but in some instances the alarms may come back. Further improvements were added to allow Symbio to wait for the controller to fully reboot and then validate the alarm before passing it to the event log. 

 

Product Family

Voyager 3 units built in Clarksville, TN

Precedent units built in Clarksville, TN & Lynn Haven, FL

 

Model Prefix(s) & Configuration details

All units with modulating gas heat (model number digit 10 = B) w/ version 7.02 firmware or older. 

 

What actions can I take now:

  • Update Symbio 700 firmware to the latest version
  • Symbio™ 700 - Firmware Update for Unit Controller Module

Is there a related published service bulletin for this issue?

Are WMS(Warranty Management System) claims allowed for this issue?

  • Yes, final resolution has been confirmed per above.
