Odyssey with Symbio 700 Supply Fan Start/Stop to UCCA AHU with 1W1 Board and Contactor Started Fan

OdysseyAirflow / FansVFD / DrivesWiring / Wire PairingInstallation / StartupHeatingManuals / Literature
Section: Installation / Application R-410A [LCU]  •  Category: Light Commercial Rooftops | Splits
Created: 2025-08-29  •  Last updated: 2025-08-29

Symbio 700 has the ability to send a 24vac supply fan start/stop that can be used to energize a relay or contactor. When pairing to a UCCA AHU with the 1W1 board and NO supply fan VFD (contactor controlled fan), a field installed relay is used at the 1W1 to isolate the 24vac between the two units. 

Fan contactor on UCCA AHU with 1W1 board are wired to the 1W1 and controlled by Relay K2 which is energized by the MC port on TB6-7.
