Simplified or reduced wiring setup:

For pairing applications where control is provided by the thermostat, wiring between the air handler and condenser is reduced to a single cable for both condenser cooling and heat pump applications. In these applications, the thermostat provides control of the split system. The Symbio™ 700 accepts the Y1, Y2 (optional), O (heat pump only), and Limit/Shed (compressor disable) inputs from the thermostat/AHU.

Additional Notes for Simplified Wiring:

  • Supply fan will not operate in test mode on the condenser. The supply fan control will need to be provided from the thermostat. 
  • Electric heat will not operate for heat pump in defrost mode. IMC communication wiring and an Indoor Options Module kit is required for the Symbio 700 to operate electric heat in defrost.
