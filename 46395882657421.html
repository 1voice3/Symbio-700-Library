Upgrade Symbio 700 to Advance - (Contractors & Customers Only)

General Symbio 700General
Section: Symbio  •  Category: Licensing / Registration (Software & Hardware)
Created: 2026-06-04  •  Last updated: 2026-10-08

Scenario: A Trane unit was sold without a licensed feature. An upgrade (Advance License) has been purchased, and the license serial number is available.

Follow these steps to register the Advance License to a Symbio 700 controller and download the license file:

 

  1. Launch Salesforce portal and click on [Sign In with SSO] button
  2. Login with the credentials provided

  1. In Home page search, enter the Symbio 700 serial number and press ENTER.

    Note: You can also search Asset using equipment serial number, sales order number, or job name.

  2. From the search results, open the Asset by clicking on Asset Name
  1. Verify controller serial number and equipment serial number on the Asset.

  2. Click on [View License] button, verify that no associated licenses are listed. Click [Close]

    Note: If a License Entitlement is already assigned, there is no need to add a new license. The Symbio 700 already has an Advanced License.

  3. Click on [Add License] button

  4. In Add License dialog, enter the Oracle Reference number (Order number) and  License serial number from the electronic delivery of license email and press [Search] button

    Note: Be sure to enter the exact Order Number and License Serial Number to find the license.

  5. Select the license entitlement, click [Assign] button and then Click [Done]

  6. In Asset Detail page, click on [View License] again and verify that assigned License entitlement is listed

  7. Click [Generate License File] button
  8. Generated license file (*.lic) is listed at the top under 'License Files', click on 'Downloads' link next to the license file
