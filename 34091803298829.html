Symbio™ 700 - How to format USB drive to FAT32

Created: 2025-02-06  •  Last updated: 2026-10-16

Overview

Important

  • Formatting the USB drive will erase all existing data, so back up anything you need.

There are also alternative methods to format a USB drive to FAT32, depending on your operating system.

The FAT32 format is available on USB drives with a capacity of 32GB or less.

Formatting Steps

Step 1 - Insert the USB drive and then left-click on the Windows icon on the lower left side

Step 2 - Left-click  on This PC 

 

Step 3 - Look at the devices and drives; you should see a USB drive 

Step 4 - Right-click on the USB drive; a window will pop up

 

 

Step 5 - Find Format in the window that popped up

 

Step 6 - Go to File System, go to the drop-down menu, and choose FAT32

Step 7 - Once FAT32 is selected, click on Start and follow the steps after
