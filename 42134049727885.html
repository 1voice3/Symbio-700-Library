How do I wire a CO2 Sensor for Symbio 700 and Reliatel Controls?

CO2 Sensor

 

Recommended Wire

Analog input wire must be 18-gauge shielded control wire, 3 conductors

Sensor Information

Trane offers two versions of CO2 sensors:

  • Duct Mount X13790423010 or SEN03074
  • Wall Mount X13790422010 or SEN01564

Both sensors utilize the same internal circuit board and connection and only differ in their mounting and sensor pickup location. Both are capable of 0-10vdc, 2-10vdc, 4-20mA with a range of 0-2000ppm. 

Info

For all Symbio 700 and ReliaTel units, 0-10vdc input is utilized.

Sensor Wiring

For Symbio 700 and ReliaTel applications, the following terminals are used:

  • 24v
  • GND
  • OUT (older sensors may show OUT1 or OUT V)

 

Symbio 700 Connections

The CO2 connection on the Symbio 700 is at the bottom right corner on J22:

Drain wire can be connected on J22-3 with the ground wire. 

ReliaTel Connections

The CO2 connection on ReliaTel units are on LTB2:

Drain wire can be connected on LTB2-14 with the ground wire.
