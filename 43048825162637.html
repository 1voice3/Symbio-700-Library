Symbio® 700 V9.00.0007 (September 2026)

When to update Firmware: Guidelines and Best Practices for Updating Firmware

 

Voyager 3™ 27.5-50 Tons, 60 Hz and 22.9-41.7 Tons, 50 Hz

New/Improved Features Supported:

  • No change from the previous release

Field reported items addressed:

  • None listed

 

Precedent™ 3-25 Tons

New/Improved Features Supported:

  • Support for field-provided Hot Water Coil valve control
  • Support for Modulating Electric Heat across all tonnages
  • Support for Secondary Heat Outdoor Air Temperature Lockout Setpoint functionality
  • Support for Heating Capacity Primary Limit and Heating Capacity Secondary Limit
  • Enhanced Heating Remote Capacity Control Scheme
  • Improvement to dehumidification mode sequence when using a humidistat; exit criteria now include the Dehumidification Disable Space Temperature Setpoint.
  • Improved reheat valve sequencing during dehumidification for a more responsive comfort control sequence.
  • Enabled Pre-Cool and Morning Warm-up by default.
  • Improved Supply Air Tempering transitions after dehumidification operation.
  • Improvement to Heat Pump Heating Switchover functionality for more consistent Occupied and Unoccupied operation.
  • Improve Symbio 700 support for TGP2 programs.
  • Addition of Relief Fan Status datalog into the standard factory trends.

Field reported items addressed:

  • Improved unit response during IMC communication events when used with supported factory smoke detector or ventilation override configurations.
  • Improved SC+ graphic support for heat pump-configured applications.
  • BACnet IP network number default value changed to "0".
  • Fixed Air-Fi zone sensor interaction for supply fan configuration.
  • Improved VVDA unit operation for 3rd party relay-controlled occupied/unoccupied applications.
  • Improvement to Supply Fan operating during fan-only mode.
  • Fixed supply fan sequencing during unoccupied heating operation for CVZT and VVZT unit types.
  • Fixed alarm communication to Tracer SC+ through LonTalk.
  • Addressed condensate overflow response issue for VVDA units with invalid Space Temperature Active.
  • Improvement to reversing valve control for heat pump units operating out of conventional thermostats.
  • Fixed Non-thermostat Tempering control sequence to better maintain discharge air temperature target.
  • Fixed supply fan operation during occupied-to-unoccupied transitions for heat pump units.
  • Fixed status reporting economizer decision method to align with configured economizer type.
