Relay Logic Circuit for a 24 VAC output in Heat Pump Defrost Mode (Symbio 700)

 

Overview:

This circuit will provide a 24 VAC output from a Symbio 700 Heat Pump Condenser in Defrost Mode.

You will need to purchase 2 Relays, one must have Normally Closed contacts, and the other must-have Normally Open contacts.

Light Commercial Tech Support will not be responsible for providing part numbers for the relays.

 

 

Theory of Operation

If the Symbio 700 is calling for a compressor to run, and not calling for a condenser fan to run, the unit is in Defrost Mode...so output 24 VAC.

 

Referring to the diagram above:

Unit Idle

R1 = Coil Not Energized, Contacts Closed

R2= Coil Not Energized, Contacts Open

OFC1 coil, R1 coil, and CC1 coil are not energized, no 24VAC is present to energize Relay R2 coil, and 24 VAC from AB J15-1 cannot pass through R2 contacts.

Cool or Compressor Heat Mode

R1 = Coil Energized, Contacts Open

R2= Coil Not Energized, Contacts Open

OFC1 and R1 coils energized (opening R1 Contacts) not allowing 24 VAC energizing CC1 coil to energize R2 coil. 24 VAC from AB J15-1 cannot pass through R2 contacts.

Defrost Mode 

R1 = Coil Not Energized, Contacts Closed

R2= Coil Energized, Contacts Closed

24 VAC energizing CC1 coil passes through R1 Normally Closed contacts to energize R2 Coil (closing R2 contacts), 24 VAC from AB J15-1 passes through R2 contacts.
