Can I use a regular thermostat in conjunction with a programmable zone sensor?

PrecedentVoyagerOdysseyWiring / Wire PairingSensors / ModulesInstallation / StartupHeating
Section: Trane Thermostats  •  Category: Light Commercial Rooftops | Splits
Created: 2020-01-08  •  Last updated: 2026-07-22

Reliatel

No, a unit with a Programmable Zone Sensor connected to the RTRM module J6 will simply ignore the thermostat outputs.  This includes Precedent, Odyssey, and Voyager units.

Note: To use a thermostat, only the wiring to the RTRM moduleJ7 is required.

 

Symbio 

No, a unit with a Programmable Zone Sensor connected to the Symbio unit controller UC-J19 will ignore the thermostat output. The UC module Space Controller is configured for a Single Setpoint Zone Sensor or a Dual Setpoint Zone Sensor. This includes 3 - 25 Tons Precedent units.

Note: To use a thermostat, configure the Space Controller to Conventional T-Stat.
