Service Alert: Voyager 3 - (Symbio 700) - Transformer Pigtail Connection Issues

Service Alert Summary:

Transformer pigtail connectors damaged from manufacturer.

Info about Service Alerts

What are known Diagnostics or Symptoms:

Loose connections have been found on both primary and secondary transformer connections on all 24vac transformers that have been tied back to the manufacturer. 

 

Affected connectors:

  • Primary transformer connector(s) PPM/F 6/7/8/9
  • Secondary transformer connector(s) PPM/F 1/2/3/4

Loose transformer connections are known to cause:

  • compressor proving faults
  • condenser fan operation issues
  • blower motor issues (A0 cabinet)
  • potential sensor failures
  • IMC communication issues

Product Family

Voyager 3 w/ Symbio 700 controls

Model Prefix(s) & Configuration details

See product families above, all models affected

Ship Date range 

1-1-2023 - Present

Serial range 

All - Present

What actions can I take now: 

Remove connector and modify harness to connect directly to the transformer.

  • Preferred connectors for the primary side are:
    • TE 3-520150-2 Quick Disconnect Receptacle, Fully Insulated, 16 – 14 AWG, 3/16" Tab, Blue or equivalent
  • Preferred connectors for the secondary side are:
    • TE 3-520140-2 Quick Disconnect Receptacle, Fully Insulated, 16 – 14 AWG, 1/4" Tab, Blue or equivalent
    • TE 2-520183-2 Quick Disconnect Receptacle, Fully Insulated, 22 – 18 AWG, 1/4" Tab, Red or equivalent
