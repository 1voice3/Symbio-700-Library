Download the Symbio Service & Installation Mobile Phone App

With the Symbio Service & Installation app, users can conveniently access and set up the main Symbio Unit Controller Module from their mobile devices. The user can download and install the app on their smartphone using the links below.

 

Video

 

 

 

 

Instructions

  • To download the Symbio Service & Installation mobile app:
  1. Navigate to the Apple App Store or Google Play Store on your mobile device.
  2. Search for Trane Symbio 700 to locate the Symbio Service & Installation app.
  3. Download and install the app.

 

 

Apple

https://apps.apple.com/us/app/symbio-service-installation/id1309310176

 

 

Google Play

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trane.mobileservicetool&hl=en_US&gl=US&pli=1
