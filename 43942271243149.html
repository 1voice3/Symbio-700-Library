Transfer an existing license to a new Symbio 700 controller - Salesforce (External Users Only)

General Symbio 700Firmware / SoftwareInstallation / Startup
Section: Symbio  •  Category: Licensing / Registration (Software & Hardware)
Created: 2026-03-02  •  Last updated: 2026-06-01

Scenario: A Trane unit with an Advance Symbio 700 is installed at a customer site. The existing Symbio 700 has failed and must be replaced. A replacement controller (unlicensed) has been purchased.

Follow these steps to transfer the Advance License to the new Symbio 700 controller and download the license file:

 

  1. Log in to Salesforce Portal

  2. In Home page search, enter the Symbio 700 serial number and press ENTER.

    Note: You can also search Asset using equipment serial number, sales order number, or job name.

  3. From the search results, open the Asset by clicking on Asset Name
  4. Verify controller serial number and equipment serial number on the Asset.

  5. Click on [Transfer License] button

  6. In the Transfer License dialog, enter the new Symbio 700 controller serial number and click Finish.
  1. verify that the Controller Serial Number is updated and the Status has changed to “Regenerate License” in the Asset Details
  1. Click [Generate License File] button
  2. Generated license file (*.lic) is listed at the top under 'License Files', click on 'Donwloads' link next to the license file
