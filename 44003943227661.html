Installation Instructions Manual

Click the e-library symbol for the latest version of the 0 to 50% Motorized Outside Air Damper.

 

 

Wiring

  • Using the supplied harness, connect PPF87 to the actuator connector.
  • Route the harness to the fresh air options module located in the return section and connect to the blue connector to P11 and the black connector to P8.

 

 

 

Configuration

  • Click the link below to configure or edit the configurations.
    •  https://support.trane.com/hc/en-us/articles/16561370756237-Symbio-700-How-to-configure-or-edit-configurations

Phone App

  • Click Settings > View Configuration > Edit > Proceed, then edit the required configurations.

 

Using the Symbio 700 Onboard menu and keypad

  1. Press Home.
  2. Press the Down Arrow and select Utilities.
  3. Press the Check.
  4. Press the Down Arrow and select Unit Config.
  5. Press the Check.
  6. Press the Down Arrow and select Edit Unit Config.
  7. Press the Check.
  8. Press the Down Arrow, then select Outside Air.
  9. Press the Check.
  10. Press the Down Arrow, and select 0 to 50% Motorized Damper.
  11. Press the Down Arrow, then verify that the Remote Minimum Position is Not Installed.
  12. Or Press the Check.
  13. And select Not Installed.
  14. Press the Check.
  15. Press the Down or Up Arrow until you find Save Config.
  16. Press the Check.
  17. Press the Down Arrow to change No to Yes.
  18. Press the Check.
  19. It will say Edit Unit Config Updating.
