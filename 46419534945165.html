Resolved Service Alert: Symbio 700 - Precedent Dual Compressor Heat Pump SOV Not Shifting when Running Compressor 2 in Cooling

PrecedentCompressor / Compressor ProvingTroubleshootingBulletins / Service AlertsFirmware / SoftwareSensors / ModulesInstallation / StartupHeating
Section: Resolved Precedent Service Alerts  •  Category: Field Service Alerts / Known Product Issues
Created: 2026-06-05  •  Last updated: 2026-10-02

 

Service Alert Summary:

Field is reporting SOV is bypassing due to not fully shifting when running Compressor 2 in cooling on dual compressor heat pump with running version 8 or lower firmware and controlled by thermostat. In version 7.02, the compressor staging sequence was changed to start Compressor1 on the cooling call to generated enough differential pressure across the SOV to force it to completely shift. Subsequent cooling cycles will start Compressor 2 as the SOV is supposed to stay energized. It has been found that with W1/O and G energized together, the unit Heat Cool Mode switches to FAN ONLY causing the SOV to drop from Symbio 700 when the cooling cycles ends, this would require Compressor 1 to start again on the next cooling cycle and it does not per sequence.
