Odyssey A2L Pairing Z: Two Odyssey TTA Condensers to one Odyssey Air Handler, 15th digit = C

OdysseyAirflow / FansWiring / Wire PairingRefrigeration / Charging
Section: Wiring  •  Category: Light Commercial Rooftops | Splits
Created: 2026-03-02  •  Last updated: 2026-08-06

Important Notes:

  • Heat pump condensers are not recommended
  • Isolation relays are required to separate the transformer voltages from each condenser
  • Any air handler with model number 15th digit = D will need to be modified to a "C" two speed unit. Below is a link for the modification.
    • Odyssey Symbio A2L R454B Air Handler Conversion from Digit 15 = D to Digit 15 = C
  • Test mode function will not bring on high speed fan operation. 
    • Y2 terminal on the Relay Board will need to have 24 vac before the supply fan will run in high speed in cooling
  • Refrigerant lines on the two condensers must not be manifolded into one refrigerant line.
